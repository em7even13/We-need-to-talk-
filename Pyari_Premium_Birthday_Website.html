04 / Everything I mean

Happy birthday, Pyari. ❤️

I don't know if a website can ever be enough to explain everything I feel, but I wanted to make something that came from me—not something I simply bought or copied. So if you're reading this, I hope you take your time with it.

There are so many things I could say about you, but the simplest one is this: I'm genuinely grateful that you became a part of my life. Even with the distance between us, you've managed to become someone whose presence feels close to me in ways I can't properly explain.

I remember the little things more than the big things. The random conversations. The moments when we stayed longer than we planned. The things you said without realizing I'd remember them. Somehow, those ordinary moments became some of my favourite memories.

I don't want this birthday to be only about wishing you another year. I want it to remind you that somewhere, someone is quietly thankful that you exist. Someone who notices the little things, cares about your happiness, and hopes you keep becoming the person you want to be.

I wish I could be there beside you today. Since I can't, I made this instead—a small place where you can come back whenever you want and remember that this was made especially for you.

No matter how far apart we are, I hope you always remember this: you matter to me. More than a birthday message can fit into words.

So today, smile a little more. Enjoy your day. Make beautiful memories. And please remember that you are deeply appreciated, cared for, and loved.

Happy birthday again, Pyari. ❤️

— Always, me.
